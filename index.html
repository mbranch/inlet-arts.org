Our Mission

"Just as inlets connect deep land to open sea, we bring dance into communities that have been out of reach — as a vehicle for human connection, community building, and embodied education."

Inlet Arts / Dance. Access. Education. is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization based on Seattle's Eastside. We produce and present contemporary dance, provide professional artist development, and bring dance education to schools and communities across the greater Seattle region.
