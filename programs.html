Youth · After-School Programming

Dance Blast!

Inlet Arts' after-school dance program for grades K–5. Learn moves from pop culture, build your own choreography, and close every class with a dance jam: music, lights, full energy.

Sessions run seven to ten weeks and culminate in an open showing for families. Our approach centers exploration, physicality, and joy — giving kids the chance to move, invent, and make dance their own. Scholarships are available.

Meet the Teachers

Kendall Imus

Kendall holds a BA in Dance from the University of Washington. She has been dancing since age 7 and enjoys a wide range of styles. She is co-director of Orange County Peace Camp, an annual summer program for children ages 3–18 in California, and works on Seattle film sets in the camera and lighting departments.

Arianna Texeira

Arianna holds a BA in Dance and Education from the University of Washington. With years of experience teaching dance, preschool, and elementary school, she is passionate about bringing dance and education together and continues her work as a dance artist and educator in the Seattle area.

Chloe Dylla

Chloe is a dancer and educator trained in ballet, jazz, and contemporary dance. She holds a BA in Dance and Political Science from the University of Washington and brings a commitment to inclusive, inspired learning to every class she teaches.

Community · Year-round

Experience Dance Program

Free lecture-demonstrations, movement classes, and open studio sessions woven through all Inlet Arts programming — in partnership with community groups across the Eastside.

Past venues include South Bellevue Community Center, Bellevue Art Museum, Crossroads Community Center, and Eastside libraries. 1,000+ participants served.

Intensive · Summer 2027

RISE

An intimate two-week intensive pairing emerging dancers with choreographers and educators from Seattle and the national field. Set in the beauty of the Pacific Northwest, with a public culminating performance.
